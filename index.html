                Nothing is ever removed on its own — goals stay until you delete them, even after a period ends. In Today's Goals, the square on the right ranks importance: the first one you tick becomes 1, the next 2, and unticking closes the gap. In Obligations, the square on the right just flags what matters: click it once and it turns green, again and it turns blue, a third time and it clears. Either way the goal jumps to the top of its list — blue ones above green ones — ranked ones in rank order — and it never completes or removes the goal. The clock on the left of each goal in Today's Goals sends it over to Not Today. The phone button marks who you've contacted. Obligations and Big Goals each run on one date range you set: click the date button, pick a start day then an end day, or take a preset. Double-click any goal to rename it. Enter adds, Esc cancels. Projects: + New in the Projects bar makes one; double-click (or right-click) a project to rename it, change its colour or delete it. The tag button on a goal puts it in or out of projects. Clicking a project shows only its goals, and while it is on, new goals go into it. A goal carried a week or more turns solid orange: do it, park it, or delete it. Every section has its own tab along the top — Ctrl+1…0 jumps between them. The Week tab reads back what you did and what slipped; on Sunday evening it opens by itself with the week's brief. on the Board you can drag a box by its title to rearrange. Your thought clouds live on the Thoughts tab. Alt+N takes you there with a fresh cloud. A cloud stays until you empty it or press its ×.
